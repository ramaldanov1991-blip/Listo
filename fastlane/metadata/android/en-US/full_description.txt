Listo is a simple, beautiful sticky-notes app that lives right on your home screen.

<ul>
<li>Home screen note widgets in 4 sizes</li>
<li>Bold, italic, underline and strikethrough</li>
<li>Bulleted lists, 7 note colours, search, autosave</li>
<li>Dark theme</li>
</ul>

No sign-up, no ads, no data collection and no internet permission — your notes stay on your phone.
